## Limits (no overclaiming)
* Single global chart `ℝ^d`; no atlas, no transition maps, no compact manifolds; the sphere in stereographic coordinates is not covered (incomplete chart: `g_exh` fails because the pole is at finite distance).
* `g_exh` is a sufficient criterion for completeness; equivalence with geodesic completeness (Hopf–Rinow/Greene–Wu smoothing) not formalised.
* The Riemannian curvature objects are the book's coordinate formulas (Mathlib has no curvature tensor); the identification with an intrinsic Riemann tensor is not formalised.
* `hypRiem` is checked against the geometry by reproducing `K = -1` through the library's own formulas, not by an independent comparison with a Mathlib hyperbolic-plane object (Mathlib has none in this version).
