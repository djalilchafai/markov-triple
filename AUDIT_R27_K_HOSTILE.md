1. MEDIUM (doc, fixed): what `goFull_isFellerC0` says. `IsFellerC0 T` (R24/Operator.lean) is: for all t, f in C_c(X), u in L^2(mu) with u = f mu-a.e., there is g in C_0(X) with P_t u = g mu-a.e. It does not require `IsOpenPosMeasure`. With X = R and goMu supported on [-1,1] (density 3/4 (1-x^2) on (-1,1)) the statement is NOT vacuous: any continuous function on [-1,1] extends to C_c(R) (Tietze), and any g in C_0(R) restricts to a function continuous on the closed interval, which is the unique (mu has full support on (-1,1), and the closed-interval version is determined by its values on (-1,1)) continuous version. So it is equivalent to "P_t maps classes of C([-1,1]) to classes having a version in C([-1,1])", endpoints included (the Lean proof builds a uniform limit on [-1,1], via the clamp and cut-off `chi1`). The a.e.-class degeneracy outside [-1,1] only makes the C_0-at-infinity requirement trivial; it does not weaken the inside. However it is weaker than the Feller property of a Markov process: class-level on L^2(mu), no kernels (R24 `Qop`/`fellerKernelInf` need `IsOpenPosMeasure`, formalised as failing: `goMu_not_isOpenPos`), no pointwise semigroup, no sup-norm strong continuity. The docs say most of this honestly (STATEMENTS Status, GAPS "kernels STILL OPEN") but ERRATA R27-K-2 was labelled HIGH/headline with the informal "equivalently ..." unqualified. Relabelled with a qualification in ERRATA R27-K-2 (not deleted).
