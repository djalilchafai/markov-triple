## 1. What was proved (route = the book's, App. C.3)

* **Definitions (coordinate formulas only)**: `gl` = entries of `(g^{ij})⁻¹` (App. C.3: "`G*` the inverse matrix, the
  co-metric"); `chr` = Thm C.3.2 `γ_{ij}^k = ½ g^{kp}(∂_i g_{jp} + ∂_j g_{ip} − ∂_p g_{ij})`; `hess` = `∇_i w_j = ∂_i w_j −
  γ_{ij}^k w_k` with `w = du`; `riem i j k l = R_{ij}{}^k{}_l` with `(∇_i∇_j − ∇_j∇_i) Z^k = R_{ij}{}^k{}_l Z^l`, i.e.
  `∂_iγ_{jl}^k − ∂_jγ_{il}^k + γ_{ip}^kγ_{jl}^p − γ_{jp}^kγ_{il}^p`; `ricci i j = Σ_k R_{ki}{}^k{}_j` (C.3.1);
  `beRicci = ricci + hess W`.
* **Order of the proof** (as in the book): (i) calculus of the inverse matrix (`pderiv_g_eq`, Jacobi's formula
  `pderiv_detg` proved from `det_apply'` + `cramer`; Mathlib has none); (ii) `∇ g^{ij} = 0` (`pderiv_g_chr`), `Σ_bγ_{ib}^b =
  ∂_i log w_g`, hence `L u = g^{ij}∇_i∇_ju − g^{ij}∂_iW∂_ju` (`L_eq_hess`); (iii) first-order identity
  `∂_kΓ(u,v) = Σ g^{ij}(∇_k∇_iu ∂_jv + ∂_iu ∇_k∇_jv)` (`pderiv_GamF`); (iv) `∇∇Γ(f) = 2g^{ab}∇_iH_{ja}f_b + 2g^{ab}H_{ja}H_{ib}`
  (`hess_GamF`), `∂_l(Lf)` (`pderiv_L`); (v) Ricci identity `∇_iH_{aj} − ∇_aH_{ij} = −R_{ia}{}^p{}_j f_p` (`cov3_ricci`;
  uses commutation of third derivatives); (vi) antisymmetry of the lowered `R` in its last pair (`riem_antisymm`; second
  derivatives of `g_{ij}`) which turns the contraction into `Ric`; (vii) assembly `riem_bochner` through the pure-algebra
  lemma `alg_assemble`.
* Index gymnastics is isolated in `CurvAlg.lean` (pure `Fin d` sums) with the tactics `sums2/3/4` of `CurvSum.lean`
  (permutation lemmas `p3_*`, `p4_*` generated by script, each checked by the kernel; the tactic only tries them and closes

## 2. Attacks on the statements, and results

### 2.1 Vacuity / satisfiability
* `RiemData d` is inhabited (`ofGibbs`, `diagRiem`, and now `confRiem`).  The hypothesis of `riem_curv_of_ricci` is
  satisfiable with `ρ > 0` on a non-flat datum (`confRiem.curv`: `Ric + ∇∇W = (4e^{|x|²}(1+|x|²) − 2)δ ≥ 2g`) and the
  conclusion is not trivially true (`gauss_not_curv_two`: `Curv 2` fails for the standard Gaussian, so `Curv ρ` really
  constrains `ρ`).
* `riem_bochner` is an equality of two real numbers for every `f` with `Sm f`; `Sm f` is needed (`pderiv` is `0` off
  differentiable points) and is hypothesised.  It is not a statement about a single hand-picked `f`.
* `d = 0`: all sums empty, the identity reads `0 = 0 + 0` (`Γ₂ = 0`); the hypothesis of `riem_curv_of_ricci` is
  `ρ·0 ≤ 0` (true for every `ρ`), and `Curv ρ` indeed holds for every `ρ` (`Γ = Γ₂ = 0`: no directions).

### 2.2 Conventions (co-metric `g^{ij}` vs metric `g_{ij}`)
* `RiemData.g` is the CO-metric (BGL p. 46-47, App. C.3).  The metric `gl` is its matrix inverse; `det` of the co-metric
  is positive (`detg_pos`), so `Matrix.inv` is not junk.  `chr` uses `g^{kp}` times first-kind symbols of `gl`
  (`chr1 q i j = ½(∂_i g_{jq} + ∂_j g_{iq} − ∂_q g_{ij})`) exactly as Thm C.3.2.  Cross-check `gl_chr`
  (`Σ_k g_{qk}γ_{ij}^k = chr1`) and `pderiv_gl_chr1` (`∂_kg_{pq} = γ_{q,kp} + γ_{p,kq}`).
* `hess u i j = pderiv (pderiv u j) i − Σ γ_{ij}^k ∂_k u`: outer derivative index `i`; symmetric (`hess_symm`) thanks to
  `pderiv_symm` and `chr_symm`.  Symmetry of the Christoffel symbols in the lower indices (`chr_symm`) is proved, not assumed.
* `riem i j k l`: first two indices are the derivative indices `(i,j)`, `k` is the upper index, `l` the argument; the book's
  `R_{ij}{}^k{}_l`.  `ricci i j = Σ_k riem k i k j` is (C.3.1) `Ric_{ij} = R_{ki}{}^k{}_j` verbatim.  The weighted tensor is
  `Ric + Hess W` with the SAME `chr` for `W` (a smooth scalar), as in (1.16.4).
* `Ric ≥ ρ g` in `riem_curv_of_ricci` is the quadratic-form inequality on vectors `ξ ∈ ℝ^d` (upper index): `ρ Σ g_{ij}ξ^iξ^j ≤
  Σ (Ric+∇∇W)_{ij}ξ^iξ^j` (BGL p. 508: "the tensor `Ric − ρg` is positive").  Then `ξ = ∇f` (`grad f i = Σ_k g^{ik}∂_kf`),
  `g_{ij}∇^if∇^jf = Γ(f)` is `Gam_eq_gl_grad`.
* `‖Hess f‖²_HS := Σ g^{ik}g^{jl}H_{ij}H_{kl}`, nonnegative for ANY array `H` (`hessNorm_nonneg`, Gram decomposition of the
  positive definite `g^{ij}` through `CFC.sqrt`).

### 2.3 Sign conventions of Ricci; independent check outside Lean
A `sympy` script (scratchpad, not part of the library) implements the same formulas (`chr1`, `chr`, `riem`, `ricci`) and gives
`Ric = +g` for the round sphere (stereographic chart `4/(1+r²)²`), `Ric = −g` for the hyperbolic half plane `1/y²`, and
`Ric = −2·I` for `confRiem` (`G = e^{|x|²}I`), in agreement with `ricci_eq` and with the Gauss curvature `K = −e^{-2φ}Δφ`
(`φ = |x|²/2`): `Ric = K g = −2 e^{-|x|²}e^{|x|²} I`.  The same script verifies `Γ₂ = ‖H‖² + (Ric+∇∇W)(∇f,∇f)` symbolically for
`confRiem` and a generic `f(x,y)` (residual 0).  The Lean theorem proves it for all data; the sign of `Ric` in `riem_bochner`
is forced by the theorem itself (a wrong sign makes the statement false).

### 2.4 Hand checks in dimension 1 and 2
* `d = 1`: `riem i i k l = 0` (`riem_self`, antisymmetry in the derivative pair is manifest from the definition), so
  `ricci = 0` (`ricci_fin_one`): every one-dimensional chart is flat (BGL p. 508: "in dimension one there is only one metric after a change of
  variables").  Bochner then reads `Γ₂ = (g^{11})² H² + ∇∇W (∇f)²` with `H = f'' + ½ (a'/a) f'`, `a = g^{11}`.  For `a ≡ 1`
  this is the Euclidean (1.16.5) (see the flat check).
* Flat check: `ofGibbs G` has `gl = δ`, `chr = 0`, `riem = 0`, `ricci = 0`, `hess = ∂∂`; `ofGibbs_bochner` shows that
  `riem_bochner` reduces to the right-hand side of `gibbsRd_Γ₂_eq` (BGL (1.16.5)).
* `d = 2`: `confRiem` (above).  Round sphere / hyperbolic plane are NOT `RiemData` (stereographic co-metric `(1+r²)²/4·I`
  is unbounded above, the half plane is not `ℝ²`), so they cannot be inhabited in Lean; they were checked in `sympy` only.

### 2.5 Junk values, partial functions
`pderiv` is `0` off differentiable points: all derivative identities assume `Sm` of the function (`hu`, `hf`); `chr`,
`riem`, `ricci` are built from `pderiv` of smooth functions (`sm_gl`, `sm_chr1`, `sm_chr`).  `Real.rpow` in `wg`: base
`det > 0`.  `Finset` sums over `Fin d` (no `Fintype` junk).  `Matrix.inv` (junk iff `det = 0`): `detg_pos`.

### 2.6 What is NOT claimed
* `WeakHypo` (elliptic regularity), `F8` are still named hypotheses; Poincaré, spectral gap, `L²` decay, Full triple, `CD`,
  LSI for `confRiem` (`confRiem_poincare`, ...) hold only under them.  `confRiem.curv` and `riem_bochner` are unconditional.
* Only the single global chart `ℝ^d` (co-metric bounded above, finite measure); no atlas, no intrinsic manifold; no
  Riemannian-distance statement.
* `CD(ρ,n)` with finite `n` (needs `Ric ≥ ρ g` plus a dimension term) is not addressed; `Curv ρ` is `n = ∞`.
* The docstring of `Riemannian/Main.lean` still says "NOT proved" (existing file, not edited; ERRATA R16-1).
